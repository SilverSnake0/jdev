BUSINESS + INTERNAL PLATFORM

Likeness Labs

From a meaningful image to a model you can hold.

The business

I co-founded Likeness Labs to turn 2D images into AI-assisted 3D models and personalized, 3D-printed keepsakes. The workflow connects image intake, model generation, review, print preparation, and physical production.

The software behind production

I built internal tools to organize the work surrounding the models. The Event Operations dashboard brings product records, model and STL files, production targets, printer assignments, inventory, event tasks, and sales records into one workspace.

From generation to a finished product

Meshy supports image-to-3D generation, but a generated model is only a starting point. Model review, dimensional preparation, test printing, and quality checks are separate steps. Event workspaces keep production and inventory organized without mixing records between events.

Private operations platform. The screenshot uses an empty demo workspace; customer images, orders, financial records, and internal links are not published.
